The American Ringtail,
Tail Carried High

A display board for our American Ringtail cats — the breed whose tail curls up and over the back like a plume. Browse the frames, read the discovery notes, and meet the pairs on the board below.

I.Discovery

The American Ringtail is named for its signature feature: a tail that curls up and over the back, carried loosely like a ring rather than held straight. The curl is natural to the breed — the tail is flexible and muscular, and the cat moves it freely. It is not kinked, curled by handling, or held in place; the cat raises and relaxes it at will.

Temperament

Ringtails are generally known as friendly, curious, and people-oriented cats. They tend to follow household activity, greet visitors, and stay engaged with their families rather than keeping to themselves.

The Tail

The curled tail is the breed’s hallmark. When the cat is relaxed or moving about, the tail loops over the back; when the cat rests, it settles like any other tail. It is a natural trait passed through the line.

Care Basics

Care is the ordinary good care any cat deserves: sound nutrition, fresh water, a clean litter station, regular veterinary visits, indoor living, and daily attention and play. The curled tail needs no special handling.

III.The Society — Bonded Pairs Seeking Their Forever Homes

Some of our ringtails live together as bonded pairs — two cats who have grown up side by side and lean on each other. Those pairs were never going to stay with us forever. We have been keeping them safe and loved while we waited for their forever homes to come along, and now we are opening the door and letting those homes find them.

The pairs on this page are fully intact, and they are placed selectively — to known breeders in the papered American Ringtail world, and to genuine forever homes that are ready to take two cats together and keep the line strong. Fewer animals in our house also makes the air easier for our family’s health, and that matters to us too — but the heart of it is simple: these cats were always waiting for their people. Maybe that’s you.

Known Breeders

We welcome inquiries from known breeders working in the papered American Ringtail line. Placements to breeders help strengthen the line, and each pair is considered on its own merits.

Genuine Forever Homes

A pair may also go to a genuine forever home — a family ready to love two cats for the whole of their lives. Tell us about your home, and we will tell you honestly whether a pair fits it.

Pairs Go as Pairs

A bonded pair is placed together, always. The bond is not split — two cats who have kept each other company their whole lives go to their new home the same way: together.

If one of these pairs sounds like your home, write to us at sales@apexairsolutions.com and tell us a little about yourself. Profiles are filled in pair by pair as the roster is confirmed, so if a card above still reads “roster pending,” that pair’s page is on its way.

IV.What We’re Seeking

We are seeking known breeders in the papered TICA American Ringtail line who are interested in our intact pairs. If you work in this line and would like to be considered when the pairs are placed, we invite your inquiry. Tell us about your cattery, your line, and your experience with the breed — placements are made to known breeders only.
